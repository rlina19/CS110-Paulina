1 · Introduction

One useful idea is that mindset is a part of game development. My interpretation is that there are parts to game development. Mindset, structure, documentation, and the actual resources to develop your game. Question: "How does separating the work into parts help with game development?"

2 · Developer mindset

Creativity and innovation is important for game developing. It helps you think outside the box for unique ideas. Its important to persevere and have the right mindset because its important to be able to adapt to change quickly. Keep in mind who are your fans and focus on the fans. Growth mindset: take on challenges, learn from failures, effort, accept feedback, surround yourself with the right people, and don't stop learning. To stay motivated: set clear goals, lists and deadlines. Stay organized.

3 · Folders and project creation

Folder structure is important because it makes it easier to manage your project and share it with others.

4 · Documentation

Folder structure is important for documentation for everything to be organized and for new members to see your project . Question: How can we share our documents with others?

5 · Introduction to Unity

I observed how to start a project on Unity. Open a 3D project and title it the name of yor project. Its important to save this project in the folder with your project. On unity you can see all your assets and all sorts of information on the inspector. One question I have is how to actually make your game do stuff on Unity.

6 · Import a custom player

Observations: On Mixamo.com you can get 3d characters and then import them into Unity for your game. You can check your folders through Explorer ad this can also help with folder structure. You can rearrange or fix folders. A folder with your project name should be created to organize everything.
